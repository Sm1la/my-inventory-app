# My requests, photos and search

My requests filters by an immutable requester account ID assigned by the database at submission. Exact historical work emails were linked to their accounts; older Sharmila requests were linked to the known workspace administrator. Other unlinked name-only records remain in Requests.

The existing approval and stock engine is preserved. A manager's Mark ready action fulfils an approved request, issues stock once and records a ready timestamp. The requester may confirm physical collection in My requests; this records a timestamp without issuing any further stock. Earlier fulfilled records without a ready timestamp are labelled Issued · earlier record. Collection confirmation is idempotent and restricted to the live signed-in requester. The tracker is a personal view of the shared workspace, not a change to existing shared request visibility.

Inventory managers upload real product photos through Edit item. Upload, replacement and removal save immediately. A private Supabase Storage bucket accepts JPG, PNG and WebP up to 5 MB. The browser re-encodes images to JPEG at up to 1200 pixels, removing embedded metadata. Object policies enforce the active account, workspace and manager role. Staff use expiring signed URLs to view photos; links refresh while the app is open. Item thumbnails appear in inventory, ordinary request rows, receipt rows and My requests. Grouped paper/size choices also show their saved photos while retaining the grouped layout. Items without photos show a category icon. No product photos are invented or automatically assigned.

Shared search normalises accents, punctuation, colour/color spelling, Post-it/sticky notes and ballpoint/ball pen wording. All words may match across fields in any order; complete item codes may omit separators. Inventory and reports search name, code, brand, colour, supplier, location, category, packaging and type. Request and receipt pickers use the same search helper. This is deterministic matching rather than an external AI service.
